01Customisation Protocol & Delivery Governance100+ monthly requests

Challenge Inconsistent intake, ownership and delivery visibility across multiple accounts.

Action Designed a BRD-led protocol covering feasibility, backlog, capacity, seven-day sprints, acceptance criteria, UAT, feedback, rework and closure, supported by a Power BI governance dashboard.

Impact Governed 100+ monthly requests across 35 client accounts and reclaimed approximately four hours per week.

SDLC · Requirements · Sprint governance · UAT · Power BI

02Enterprise Asset Management120+ assets

Challenge Fragmented tracking across six departments limited accountability and reporting.

Action Led cross-functional workshops, mapped requirements, and shaped a Zoho Creator solution with controls, KPIs and Power BI reporting.

Impact Established a single governed view of 120+ assets across finance, IT, sales and operations.

Workshops · Process mapping · Zoho Creator · KPI design

03Public Healthcare Insurance Platform2 Spot Awards

Challenge Delivering reliable change within a complex US state-government healthcare insurance platform.

Action Analysed requirements, managed work in Jira, supported functional testing and UAT, led a four-member team and used Python/pandas to strengthen QA checks.

Impact Expanded QA coverage by 12–15% and received two Deloitte Spot Awards for contribution and team leadership.

Requirements · Jira · Testing · UAT · Team leadership

04Incident Management & CAPA~6 hours saved weekly

Challenge Incidents lacked consistent ownership, escalation, root-cause discipline and closure validation.

Action Defined severity, SLAs, ownership, escalation, RCA, corrective and preventive action, and closure controls in ClickUp.

Impact Improved traceability and accountability while reclaiming approximately six hours each week.

Incident management · RCA · CAPA · SLA · ClickUp

05Approval & Workforce Workflows13-user visibility

Challenge Manual payment approvals and fragmented attendance visibility created delays and follow-up work.

Action Designed Jotform workflows for one-time, recurring and credit-card payments, plus a whereabouts flow for office, remote, leave and holiday status.

Impact Streamlined 15–20 monthly requests, saved roughly two hours weekly and improved visibility for 13 users.

Workflow design · Approvals · Jotform · Change adoption

06Retail Intelligence Platform10,000+ daily records

Challenge A regional intelligence need required reliable data collection, validation, reporting and client delivery.

Action Led discovery and coordinated scraper/API vendors, data pipeline, database, backend, dashboard and Excel delivery teams through UAT.

Impact Enabled 10,000+ daily records across 21 retailer-country pairs and removed about 5,000 false events per day through validation improvements.

End-to-end delivery · APIs · Vendors · Data validation · UAT

07Voice of Customer & Reporting Automation~4 hours saved weekly

Challenge Feedback and recurring reporting were difficult to prioritise and laborious to prepare.

Action Built a VoC register that moved approved themes into sprint backlogs and shared enhancements, and automated Excel-to-PowerPoint reporting.

Impact Created a traceable path from feedback to delivery and saved approximately four hours each week.

VoC · Prioritisation · Backlog · Reporting automation
